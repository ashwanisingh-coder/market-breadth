How to read this dashboard
  • Overview: the market call in one line, which setups are on, the hottest themes and every stock that triggered today.
  • Market Breadth, top: indices, VIX, rupee, gold, silver, crude and FII/DII. A weak rupee, rising crude or a jump in VIX are headwinds for Indian stocks.
  • Market Breadth, below: the extra charts and the breadth-by-window tables sit at the bottom of this tab. The score (0–100) compares 10 indicators with their own last ~2 years. Under 20 = weak, 40–60 = normal, over 80 = very strong. The grid shows the share of stocks above each average; most stocks in Stage 2 = bull market.
  • Themes: sorted by Heat, so the top of the list is where money is churning today. A fresh tag plus a 3-day flow streak is the moment money starts rotating in. The file data/themes.csv is yours to edit.
  • 4% Gainers & Losers: liquid stocks that moved 4% or more today. Losers across many sectors = broad selling; a few names = stock-specific news.
  • Scanner: Setups lists the stocks ready for, or triggered in, each trade pattern, with a dot for whether that pattern is on. Screens are simple condition lists such as pullbacks to an average or stocks near highs.
  • Liquid stocks = 20-day average turnover of ₹ cr or more. Data: NSE end-of-day, and Dhan live quotes during market hours. For education, not investment advice.

Theme rotation

Click any theme to see its stocks. Heat 0–100 ranks every theme on five early signals: money flow, RS acceleration, % of stocks up 2%+, new 20-day highs and volume surges. Flow = share of today's turnover minus its usual 20-day share; Streak = days in a row that flow has been positive (3+ is real accumulation). Pure up / Proxy up = how many of each are green today — when proxies join, the theme is broadening. Universe: market cap ≥ ₹1,000 cr, no turnover filter, with thin names (under ₹2 cr a day) tagged. Edit data/themes.csv to change themes.

Scanner

My universe: market cap ≥ ₹1,000 cr, 20-day average turnover ≥ ₹ cr and price above the 200-day average. F&O marks stocks in the futures & options list. Setups are trade patterns with a trigger price; Screens are simple conditions.

🟢 trade it  ·  🟡 only A+ names, half size  ·  🔴 off. Dots turn worse the same day and better only after two days in a row. Tested on 2016–2026: breakouts and flags lost on average on red days and gained on amber and green days; green was not clearly better than amber. Ready = in the setup tonight and not triggered yet.
