Indices

✓ = price above that average. "10 > 20 rising" = short-term momentum is up. 50 / 200 DMA = the market light.

How many stocks are in uptrends

% of liquid stocks above their 20 / 50 / 200-day average. Above 50% on the 50-day = healthy. Under 20% = washed out (bottoms often form here). Above 80% = overheated.

4% winners vs 4% losers

Stocks closing 4%+ up (green, above) vs 4%+ down (red, below). Many winners across many sectors = buyers are back.

New 52-week highs vs new lows

A growing new-high list = new leaders appearing. New lows expanding = stay careful.

Net new highs (smoothed)

New highs minus new lows: 10-day average (left) and running total (right). If the index makes a new high but this line doesn't, fewer stocks are leading — a warning.

Advance / decline line vs Smallcap 250

Rising A/D line = most stocks joining the move. Index up but A/D line flat or down = narrow market (warning).

Up volume vs down volume

Money traded in rising stocks ÷ money traded in falling stocks, over 10 days. Above 1 = buying pressure. Below 1 = selling pressure.

Accumulation vs distribution days

Count in the last 25 days. Distribution = Smallcap 250 down 0.2%+ on more total turnover than the day before (big money selling). 5–6+ distribution days = market under pressure.

Volume surges

Stocks up 2%+ on 2× their normal volume — buyers stepping in. A rising count often shows up before the index turns up.

Breadth thrust

Stocks up / down 25%+ in a quarter

More stocks up 25% than down 25% = momentum trades work. When red is above green, protect capital.

Sector breadth

"vs 2 wks" = change in % above the 50-day compared with 10 trading days ago. ▲ Flip = the sector went from weak (under 40%) to strong (60%+) — leaders often come from here. Click a column to sort.

Today's 4% winners

Liquid stocks that closed 4%+ up today. "Vol ×" = today's volume vs its 50-day average (2× or more = strong demand).

How to read this page

  • The market light is the rule. Green = full size · Yellow = half size, A+ setups only · Red = no new long trades.
  • The regime meter supports it: 0–2 signals = Defensive · 3–4 = Caution · 5–6 = Constructive · 7–8 = Strong.
  • Flag radar: a rising number of tight flags = setups are building. When most recent breakouts reach +1R, flags are working — press. When most fail, cut size even if the index looks fine.
  • The meter and flag follow-through are a checklist (Proposed), not part of the rule yet.
  • Liquid stocks = 20-day average turnover of ₹ cr or more.
