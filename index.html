Themes

Click any theme to see its stocks. Heat 0–100 ranks every theme on five early signals: money flow, RS acceleration, % of stocks up 2%+, new 20-day highs and volume surges. How to read this page
  • Top line: the verdict in one sentence. Then the grid: green = most stocks in uptrends, red = most below their averages. Compare rows to see who leads.
  • Markets at a glance: a weak rupee, rising crude or a jump in VIX are headwinds for Indian stocks; gold rising while stocks fall signals fear.
  • Breadth score (0–100): 10 indicators, each compared with its own last ~2 years. Under 20 = weak (often near bottoms), 40–60 = normal, over 80 = very strong (often extended).
  • Trend + breadth together: index trending up and breadth healthy = the best backdrop. Index up but breadth falling = narrow, fragile market.
  • Stage analysis: most stocks in Stage 2 = bull market. When Stage 4 grows past Stage 2, protect capital.
  • Stock lists: four ready-made screens — pullback to the 50 EMA, pullback to the 21 EMA, within 20% of the 52-week high, and at an all-time high. Click a tab to open its list.
  • Themes: sorted by Heat, so the top of the list is where money is churning today. A fresh tag plus a 3-day flow streak is the moment money starts rotating in.
  • Themes are narrower than sectors and usually where the real money is — click a theme to see its stocks, sorted by relative strength. The file data/themes.csv is yours to edit.
  • Liquid stocks = 20-day average turnover of ₹ cr or more. Data: NSE end-of-day; rupee, crude and VIX from Yahoo Finance. For education, not investment advice.
