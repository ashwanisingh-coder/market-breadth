Supporting signals

Indices

✓ = price above that average. 10 > 20 rising = Qullamaggie's momentum check. 21 EMA = Harris / Kell. 50 / 200 DMA = my market light.

How many stocks are in uptrends

% of liquid stocks above their 20 / 50 / 200-day average. Above 50% on the 50-day = healthy. Under 20% = washed out (bottoms form here).

4% winners vs 4% losers

My mentor's habit: stocks closing 4%+ up (green, above) vs 4%+ down (red, below). Many winners across sectors = buyers are back.

New 52-week highs vs new lows

Minervini: a growing new-high list = new leaders emerging. New lows expanding = stay careful.

Advance / decline line vs Smallcap 250

Weinstein: index makes a new high but the A/D line doesn't = fewer stocks joining (warning). A/D rising while the index falls = breadth improving.

Big-picture breadth: stocks up / down 25%+ in a quarter

More stocks up 25% than down 25% = a market where momentum trades work. When the red line rises above the green one, protect capital.

Sector breadth

Which groups are strong today. Look for sectors with many stocks above the 50-day, several 4% winners and new highs: that is where the next leaders usually are. Click a column to sort.

Today's 4% winners

Liquid stocks that closed 4%+ up today. "Vol ×" = today's volume vs its 50-day average (2× or more = strong demand).

How to read this page

  • Green light + 4 or more supporting signals = full size, all setups.
  • Green light but signals weak = be selective, best setups only.
  • Yellow = half size, only A+ setups. Red = no new long trades.
  • The supporting signals are a checklist to read the market, not part of the rule yet (Proposed).
  • Liquid stocks = 20-day average turnover of ₹ cr or more.
